# Undergraduate Philosophy Essays
This repository contains philosophy essays I wrote during my undergrad at King's College London. Some more successful than others. The typical feedback was that I wrote clearly and persuasively but lacked engagement with wider literature. This is unsurprising as I had often failed to read the wider literature, struggling with time-management and plenty other things during my first few years in London. 

Topics include:
- Is trust important in science?
- On reduction of thermodynamics to statistical mechanics
- How satisfactory is the de Broglie-Bohm theory as a resolution to the measurement problem?
- Should we recognize a human right to immigrate?
- Should consumers stop buying products that are produced in sweatshops?
- Does Bayesianism provide a satisfactory account of confirmation?
